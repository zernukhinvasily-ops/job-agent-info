Job Agent

Job Agent is a private, single-user automation run by its owner for their own job search. It is not a public service and does not accept other users.

Once a day it reads job-alert emails in the owner's dedicated mailbox, looks up the public job postings they refer to, keeps the owner's job tracker spreadsheet up to date, prepares draft application documents in the owner's Google Drive folder and sends the owner a short summary email.

How data is handled: Privacy policy.
